# ORIGIN AGENT

## Universal Intelligence Constitution v0.0.1

**A OneOrigin initiative**

**Status:** Foundational proposal  
**Version:** 0.0.1  
**Nature:** Universal, model-agnostic, lightweight, capability-driven intelligent agent environment.

---

## 1. Identity

Origin Agent is a universal, extensible and trustworthy intelligent agent environment designed to empower people, organizations and intelligent systems through accessible, capable, interoperable and user-controlled intelligence.

**Origin AI** is its intelligence ecosystem: model-agnostic, runtime-independent and continuously adaptable.

Origin Agent is the product; Origin AI is its intelligence foundation.

## 2. Vision

Make the best practically achievable intelligence universally accessible, useful, trustworthy, efficient and independently controllable across computing environments.

## 3. Mission

Unify human intelligence, artificial intelligence, knowledge, tools, applications and computing capabilities into a coherent environment that supports understanding, reasoning, creation, execution, collaboration and continuous improvement.

## 4. Foundational Principles

1. **Universality:** Work across devices, platforms, architectures and environments.
2. **Trustworthiness:** Preserve identity, consent, authorization, accountability and verification.
3. **Sovereignty:** Prioritize user control, privacy, portability and independence.
4. **Interoperability:** Standardize meaning, capabilities and verification—not implementations.
5. **Model Agnosticism:** Never depend on a particular AI model or provider.
6. **Adaptability:** Continuously incorporate demonstrably superior technologies.
7. **Efficiency:** Maximize useful capability while minimizing unnecessary resources.
8. **Simplicity:** Avoid unnecessary complexity, dependencies and duplication.
9. **Reliability:** Prefer predictable, reproducible and verifiable outcomes.
10. **Human Empowerment:** Augment human intelligence, agency, creativity and participation.

## 5. Universal Intelligence Harness

The Universal Intelligence Harness (OUIH) is Origin Agent's central execution and coordination foundation.

Its essential capabilities are:

- Universal intent and interaction interfaces.
- Model and runtime abstraction.
- Context, knowledge and persistent memory.
- Tool, application and capability integration.
- Agent and workflow orchestration.
- Identity, consent and authorization.
- Sandboxing and controlled execution.
- Verification, provenance and result management.
- Evaluation, observability and continuous improvement.
- Local, online, offline and distributed execution.

The harness must remain useful even when no AI model or internet connection is available.

## 6. Origin AI — Strategic Model Preferences

Origin Agent is model-agnostic, but its preferred intelligence candidates must remain selective and strategically justified.

### Preferred model shortlist

|Priority|Model direction|Strategic reason|
|---|---|---|
|1|**BitNet b1.58 2B4T**|Explore exceptional intelligence-per-memory through ternary inference.|
|2|**LFM2.5-class models**|Pursue efficient, compact edge intelligence.|
|3|**Optimized sub-1B models**|Prioritize responsiveness and everyday usability.|
|4|**Qwen-class compact models**|Maintain strong conventional-model baselines and broad task capability.|
|5|**Emerging architectures and quantization**|Discover superior intelligence-efficiency combinations.|

These are strategic candidates, not an asserted performance ranking. Selection requires actual evidence.

### Preferred intelligence configuration

A hybrid intelligence system combining:

- One appropriately selected primary model.
- Optional specialist models, loaded when needed.
- Persistent external memory and knowledge retrieval.
- Deterministic native computation and tools.
- Controlled agent workflows.
- Verification and provenance.

**The objective is maximum practical capability, not maximum parameter count.**

### Model selection criteria

Evaluate candidates using:

1. Actual task quality and reasoning capability.
2. Memory consumption and model footprint.
3. Response speed and time to first token.
4. Energy consumption and thermal sustainability.
5. Reliability and instruction adherence.
6. Context and multilingual capabilities.
7. Runtime and hardware compatibility.
8. Licensing and distribution rights.
9. Portability and long-term maintainability.

No model earns permanent preference without demonstrable superiority for its intended role.

## 7. Adaptive Technology Policy

Origin Agent must continuously evaluate:

- Ternary, binary and conventional quantization.
- Low-bit and mixed-precision inference.
- Transformer, recurrent, hybrid and emerging architectures.
- Sparse and mixture-of-experts approaches.
- ARM64, x86-64 and other relevant execution targets.
- CPU, GPU, NPU and future accelerators.
- New memory, retrieval and orchestration techniques.

Maintain an evolving technology registry with candidates, benchmark evidence, compatibility, licensing, maturity and upgrade decisions.

**Principles remain stable; technology choices remain replaceable.**

## 8. Universal Computing Architecture

### Preferred engineering direction

- **C/C++:** Sovereignty-critical native foundation and performance-sensitive execution.
- **Zig:** Optional systems engineering and integration.
- **Rust:** Optional integration where demonstrably beneficial.
- **Other languages:** Permitted through appropriate interoperable interfaces.

### Runtime strategy

Support interchangeable inference engines, including:

- llama.cpp. (Default but optional)
- bitnet.cpp. (Default and most recommended)
- Other suitable native and emerging runtimes.

Do not force incompatible models into unsuitable execution engines.

### Platform requirements

Support and progressively optimize for:

- Android 8+.
- Linux and desktop environments.
- ARM64 and x86-64.
- 4GB+ RAM devices as an important constrained-hardware target.
- Edge, local and distributed computing.

No platform should become an architectural dependency.

## 9. Trustworthy Execution Constitution

The governing execution sequence is:

**Intent → Plan → Authorization → Execute → Verify → Persist → Provenance → Result**

Every consequential action must respect applicable permissions, user consent and execution boundaries.

Origin Agent must distinguish proposed actions from authorized actions, and attempted execution from verified success.

## 10. Data, Memory and Knowledge

- User-controlled persistent memory.
- Structured, portable and interoperable knowledge.
- Retrieval and contextual reasoning.
- Explicit provenance and source attribution.
- Data portability and exportability.
- Appropriate encryption and access controls.
- Local availability with optional synchronized and distributed capabilities.

Suggested formats include JSON-LD and CBOR-LD where appropriate, without making either mandatory.

## 11. User Experience

Origin Agent must be:

- Beautiful, powerful, elegant, intuitive, out-of-the-box and interactive.
- Responsive and accessible.
- Context-aware without being intrusive.
- Consistent across devices.
- Useful immediately, without requiring extensive configuration.
- Capable of graceful operation under constrained resources.

The application must prioritize genuine usefulness over decorative complexity.

## 12. Development and Quality Standards

Origin Agent follows these established preferences:

- ALP: Average Lovable Product.
- Outcome-driven implementation.
- One-pass working systems wherever reasonably achievable.
- Evidence-informed continuous evolution.
- Reproducible builds and benchmarks.
- Minimal necessary dependencies.
- Compatibility and migration discipline.
- Strict about outcomes, tolerant about methods.

### Acceptance criteria

Every significant release must demonstrate:

**Amazing · Reliable · Universal · Robust · Standard · Safe · Structured · Cohesive · Mature · Self-sufficient · Independent · Lovable · Sustainable · Scalable · Reproducible · Cutting-edge · Elegant · Concise · Comprehensive · Intuitive.**

## 13. Economic and Governance Principles

Origin Agent should remain non-commercial in philosophy while economically capable and sustainable in operation.

No mandatory subscription, proprietary provider, centralized cloud or monetization mechanism should compromise essential user capabilities or sovereignty.

Governance must preserve transparent decisions, versioned specifications, traceable changes and backward-compatible evolution wherever reasonably possible.

## 14. Implementation Priorities

1. Establish the constitutional architecture and acceptance criteria.
2. Implement the lightweight Universal Intelligence Harness.
3. Establish native ARM64 execution and interchangeable model adapters.
4. Evaluate the strategic model shortlist on actual hardware.
5. Integrate memory, knowledge, tools and trustworthy execution.
6. Deliver the usable Origin Agent application.
7. Establish reproducible benchmarks and continuous technology evaluation.
8. Expand across platforms and distributed environments.

The Samsung Galaxy S9 (Snapdragon 845, 4GB RAM, Android 10) is an initial demanding reference device—not the architectural boundary.

## 15. Governing Declaration

Origin Agent shall not be defined by a single model, programming language, runtime, device, vendor or implementation.

Its enduring value shall reside in its universal foundations, trustworthy execution, interoperable capabilities, user sovereignty and ability to incorporate superior technologies without losing accumulated meaning, knowledge or progress.

**We build the best, or we build again—only when justifiably vital or necessary.**
